# Case Report NDH-012: Hepatic Hemangioma (BENIGN liver tumor)
Northgate Demo Hospital | SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA, not a real patient, not medical advice
Patient: PT-33017 | 35-year-old female | Hepatology | 2025-12-03

## Summary
A 4.8 cm "enhancing liver lesion" found by accident. The enhancement pattern is the classic benign one. No biopsy was done, because hemangiomas are blood-filled and biopsy carries bleeding risk.

## Presentation
No symptoms. Ultrasound for gallstones showed a bright liver lesion. No liver disease, AFP normal (3 ng/mL).

## Imaging
- Segment 7, 4.8 cm, well-circumscribed.
- T2 MRI: markedly bright ("light bulb" sign).
- Contrast: peripheral, nodular, discontinuous enhancement in the arterial phase that fills in gradually toward the center on later phases. No washout. No capsule.
- Liver is not cirrhotic.

## Final diagnosis and category
Hepatic hemangioma. Category: BENIGN TUMOR (vascular).

## Plan
No treatment. Reassure. Repeat imaging only if symptoms or growth. Avoid biopsy.

## Why this is NOT hepatocellular carcinoma
Fill-in (centripetal) enhancement, T2 light-bulb signal, normal liver and normal AFP. Both lesions "enhance", but the pattern differs. See NDH-013.
