# Case Report NDH-006: Triple-Negative Breast Carcinoma that LOOKED benign (MALIGNANT)
Northgate Demo Hospital | SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA, not a real patient, not medical advice
Patient: PT-44102 | 52-year-old female | Breast Oncology | 2025-09-15

## Summary
A 1.6 cm round, smooth, well-circumscribed breast mass that looked like a benign fibroadenoma on ultrasound. Biopsy showed aggressive triple-negative breast cancer. Smooth borders do not exclude cancer.

## Presentation
Lump noticed 6 weeks earlier. Grew noticeably within that time.

## Imaging
- Ultrasound: round, circumscribed, hypoechoic mass with mild posterior enhancement, 1.6 cm. BI-RADS 4B.
- Axilla: one mildly thickened lymph node.

## Pathology
Invasive ductal carcinoma, no special type, grade 3. ER 0 percent, PR 0 percent, HER2 IHC 0. Ki-67 78 percent. Lymphovascular invasion not seen.

## Final diagnosis and category
Triple-negative invasive ductal carcinoma, cT1c N0 M0. Category: MALIGNANT TUMOR (cancer).

## Plan
Staging complete. Referred to Medical Oncology for systemic therapy planning before surgery.

## Why this is NOT fibroadenoma or phyllodes
Grade 3 invasive cancer cells, very high proliferation, receptor-negative profile. Fast growth plus a smooth border is a known trap.
