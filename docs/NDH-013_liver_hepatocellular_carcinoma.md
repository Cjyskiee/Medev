# Case Report NDH-013: Hepatocellular Carcinoma in cirrhosis (MALIGNANT liver tumor)
Northgate Demo Hospital | SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA, not a real patient, not medical advice
Patient: PT-28845 | 66-year-old male | Hepatitis C cirrhosis | Hepatology and Surgical Oncology | 2025-12-17

## Summary
A 3.4 cm "enhancing liver lesion" in a cirrhotic liver. Unlike the hemangioma in NDH-012, this lesion enhances brightly then washes out. In a cirrhotic liver this pattern diagnoses cancer without a biopsy.

## Presentation
Routine 6-monthly surveillance ultrasound found a new nodule. AFP 410 ng/mL. Child-Pugh A.

## Imaging
- Segment 6, 3.4 cm, well-defined.
- Arterial phase hyperenhancement, then washout in the portal venous phase, with an enhancing capsule. LI-RADS 5 (definitely hepatocellular carcinoma).
- Cirrhotic liver, no vascular invasion, no distant disease.

## Pathology
Segmentectomy specimen: moderately differentiated hepatocellular carcinoma, margins clear, no microvascular invasion.

## Final diagnosis and category
Hepatocellular carcinoma, BCLC stage A. Category: MALIGNANT TUMOR (cancer).

## Plan
Resection completed. Surveillance MRI every 3 months in year 1. Antiviral therapy continued.

## Why this is NOT a hemangioma
Washout, capsule, cirrhosis background, high AFP. Hemangiomas fill in and never wash out.
