# Case Report NDH-010: Glioblastoma (MALIGNANT primary brain tumor)
Northgate Demo Hospital | SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA, not a real patient, not medical advice
Patient: PT-49514 | 57-year-old male | Neuro-Oncology | 2025-11-12

## Summary
A 4.0 cm rim-enhancing brain mass with a dead center. Pathology showed glioblastoma, the most aggressive common primary brain cancer. It shares words like "enhancing mass" and "edema" with NDH-009 and NDH-011 but is a very different disease.

## Presentation
Three weeks of worsening headache, word-finding difficulty, left arm weakness.

## Imaging
- MRI: right frontal intra-axial mass, 4.0 cm, thick irregular rim enhancement, central necrosis, extensive FLAIR edema, crossing into corpus callosum.
- Diffusion: rim is restricted, the necrotic center is NOT strongly restricted.

## Pathology
Glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype, WHO CNS grade 4. Microvascular proliferation and palisading necrosis. MGMT promoter methylated. Ki-67 35 percent.

## Final diagnosis and category
Glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype. Category: MALIGNANT TUMOR (cancer).

## Plan
Maximal safe resection, then radiation with temozolomide (standard combined approach), then adjuvant temozolomide. Dexamethasone for edema.

## Why this is NOT meningioma or abscess
Arises inside brain tissue, necrosis with palisading, microvascular proliferation, no fever or infection source.
