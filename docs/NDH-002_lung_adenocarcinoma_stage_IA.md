# Case Report NDH-002: Lung Adenocarcinoma, Stage IA1 (MALIGNANT)
Northgate Demo Hospital | SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA, not a real patient, not medical advice
Patient: PT-51907 | 61-year-old female | 20 pack-year former smoker | Thoracic Oncology | 2025-06-02

## Summary
A 1.9 cm lung nodule that looked quiet on PET but kept growing on CT. Resection showed early invasive lung cancer. This case shares many words with the benign hamartoma (NDH-001), but the behavior over time is opposite.

## Presentation
Asymptomatic. Found during lung cancer screening CT.

## Imaging
- Right upper lobe part-solid nodule, 1.9 cm total, solid component 0.9 cm.
- Spiculated margin and pleural tag.
- Grew from 1.3 cm to 1.9 cm over 9 months.
- PET-CT: SUV 2.4 (modest, because part-solid tumors under-report on PET).

## Pathology
Invasive adenocarcinoma, acinar-predominant, invasive component 0.9 cm. Margins clear, 0 of 11 nodes involved. EGFR exon 19 deletion detected.

## Final diagnosis and category
Lung adenocarcinoma, pT1a N0 M0, stage IA1. Category: MALIGNANT TUMOR (cancer).

## Plan
VATS segmentectomy completed. No adjuvant therapy. CT surveillance every 6 months for 2 years.

## Why this is NOT a benign nodule
Interval growth, spiculation and a solid invasive component. A low PET value did not rule out cancer here.
