Title: Benign fibroadenoma of the breast in a 29-year-old woman
Author: Dr. A. Reyes

A 29-year-old woman presented with a painless, mobile lump in the left breast that she had noticed three months earlier. Ultrasound showed a well-circumscribed oval mass with smooth margins and no acoustic shadowing. Core needle biopsy reported a benign fibroadenoma with no atypia, no necrosis and no mitotic activity. The lesion was excised and histology confirmed the diagnosis. Sentinel nodes were not sampled because the imaging and histology were concordant and benign. At twelve-month follow-up the patient was well with no recurrence. Source: Synthetic Case Reports in Oncology. Location: United States. This record is fictional and contains no real patient data.
