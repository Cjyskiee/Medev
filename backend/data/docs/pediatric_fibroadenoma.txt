Title: Benign fibroadenoma of the breast in a 15-year-old girl
Author: Dr. P. Nair

A 15-year-old girl was seen with a firm, mobile, non-tender mass in the left breast that had been present for five months. Ultrasound showed a 20 mm circumscribed oval mass with smooth margins and homogeneous echotexture. Core biopsy reported a benign fibroadenoma with no atypia. Because the lesion was benign and the patient was still growing, the family chose observation rather than excision. The mass was unchanged in size at six and twelve months.
