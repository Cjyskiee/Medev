Title: Prostate adenocarcinoma in a 68-year-old man
Author: Dr. J. Whitfield

A 68-year-old man was referred with a prostate-specific antigen of 12.4 ng per mL. Digital rectal examination revealed a hard nodule in the left lobe. Magnetic resonance imaging showed a PI-RADS 4 lesion with extracapsular extension. Targeted and systematic biopsies found adenocarcinoma, Gleason score 4 plus 3, in 6 of 12 cores. Bone scan was negative. The patient underwent robot-assisted radical prostatectomy with pelvic lymph node dissection. Histology confirmed adenocarcinoma with negative margins and no nodal metastasis. Prostate-specific antigen was undetectable at three months. Source: Synthetic Urology Case Reports. Location: Australia. This record is fictional and contains no real patient data.
