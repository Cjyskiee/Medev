Title: Metformin combined with a GLP-1 agonist in adults with type 2 diabetes
Author: Dr. H. Tanaka

This randomised trial enrolled 540 adults aged 35 to 70 years with type 2 diabetes and HbA1c between 7.5 and 10.5 percent despite metformin. Participants received a weekly GLP-1 receptor agonist or placebo for 40 weeks in addition to metformin. HbA1c fell by 1.4 percentage points with the agonist and by 0.3 with placebo. Mean body weight fell by 4.6 kg versus 0.8 kg. Gastrointestinal adverse events were reported by 31 percent of the treatment group and led to discontinuation in 6 percent. No severe hypoglycaemia occurred. The trial concluded that adding the GLP-1 agonist improved glycaemic control and reduced weight in adults with type 2 diabetes. Source: Synthetic Metabolic Trials Journal. Location: Japan. This record is fictional and contains no real patient data.
