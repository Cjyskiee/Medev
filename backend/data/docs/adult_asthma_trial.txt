Title: Biologic therapy for severe asthma in adults: a randomised trial
Author: Dr. R. Okafor

This double-blind randomised trial assigned 340 adults aged 18 to 65 years with severe eosinophilic asthma to a monoclonal anti-IL-5 antibody or placebo for 52 weeks. The annual exacerbation rate was 0.8 in the treatment group and 1.9 in the placebo group. Pre-bronchodilator FEV1 improved by 210 mL with treatment and by 40 mL with placebo. Blood eosinophil counts fell by 85 percent in the treatment group. Injection-site reactions were the most common adverse event and no deaths occurred. The trial concluded that the biologic reduced exacerbations and improved lung function in adults with severe eosinophilic asthma. Source: Synthetic Respiratory Trials Journal.
