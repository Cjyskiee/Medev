Title: Invasive ductal carcinoma of the breast in a 58-year-old woman
Author: Dr. L. Cruz

A 58-year-old woman was referred after screening mammography showed a 22 mm spiculated mass with clustered pleomorphic microcalcifications in the right breast. Ultrasound confirmed an irregular hypoechoic lesion with posterior shadowing. Core biopsy reported invasive ductal carcinoma, grade 2, oestrogen receptor positive and HER2 negative. Axillary ultrasound was normal. The patient had breast-conserving surgery with sentinel node biopsy, and two of three nodes contained metastatic carcinoma. Adjuvant chemotherapy and radiotherapy were given. Source: Synthetic Case Reports in Oncology. Location: United States. This record is fictional and contains no real patient data.
