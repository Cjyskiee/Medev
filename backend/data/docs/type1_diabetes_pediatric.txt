Title: Insulin pump versus injections in children with type 1 diabetes
Author: Dr. S. Almeida

This cohort followed 96 children aged 4 to 14 years with newly diagnosed type 1 diabetes for two years. Fifty-one used a continuous subcutaneous insulin pump and 45 continued multiple daily injections. Mean HbA1c at 24 months was 7.2 percent with the pump and 8.0 percent with injections. Time in range measured by continuous glucose monitoring was 68 percent versus 55 percent. Severe hypoglycaemia occurred in two pump users and three injection users. The authors concluded that pump therapy improved glycaemic control in children with type 1 diabetes without increasing severe hypoglycaemia. Source: Synthetic Paediatric Diabetes Review.
