Title: Inhaled corticosteroids in a paediatric asthma cohort
Author: Dr. K. Lindqvist

This prospective cohort enrolled 212 children aged 6 to 11 years with mild to moderate persistent asthma. All participants started a low-dose inhaled corticosteroid with a spacer and received a written action plan. Over twelve months the annual rate of severe exacerbations fell from 0.9 to 0.3 per child, and the mean number of salbutamol rescue days per month fell from 6.4 to 2.1. Growth velocity was unchanged and no serious adverse events occurred. Adherence measured by pharmacy refill was 78 percent. The authors concluded that low-dose inhaled corticosteroids were effective in this paediatric population. Source: Synthetic Journal of Paediatric Respiratory Medicine. Location: Sweden.
