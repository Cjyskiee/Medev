Title: Benign phyllodes tumour of the breast in a 44-year-old woman
Author: Dr. M. Santos

A 44-year-old woman reported a rapidly growing, painless mass in the right breast that had doubled in size over four months. Ultrasound showed a 45 mm well-defined, lobulated, heterogeneous mass without calcification. Core biopsy suggested a fibroepithelial lesion with leaf-like architecture and mild stromal cellularity. Wide local excision with a 1 cm margin was performed. Histology confirmed a benign phyllodes tumour with low mitotic rate, no stromal overgrowth and clear margins. No adjuvant treatment was given. The patient remained recurrence free at eighteen months. Source: Synthetic Case Reports in Oncology. Location: Brazil. This record is fictional and contains no real patient data.
