Title: Benign prostatic hyperplasia treated with TURP in a 71-year-old man
Author: Dr. J. Whitfield

A 71-year-old man reported nocturia, weak stream and incomplete emptying for two years. Digital rectal examination showed a smooth, symmetrically enlarged prostate with no nodules. Prostate-specific antigen was 2.1 ng per mL. Uroflowmetry showed a maximum flow of 7 mL per second and post-void residual of 120 mL. Transurethral resection of the prostate was performed. Histology showed benign prostatic hyperplasia with no evidence of malignancy in 24 grams of tissue. At six months the symptom score had improved from 22 to 6 and the peak flow had risen to 16 mL per second. Source: Synthetic Urology Case Reports. Location: Australia.
