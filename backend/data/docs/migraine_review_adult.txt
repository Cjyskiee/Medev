Title: Triptans for acute migraine in adults: a systematic review
Author: Dr. E. Novak

This systematic review pooled 18 randomised trials of triptans for acute migraine in adults, with 12,400 participants in total. Pain freedom at two hours was achieved by 32 percent of participants taking a triptan and 12 percent taking placebo. Freedom from the most bothersome symptom at two hours was 42 percent versus 17 percent. Triptan use was associated with more chest tightness and paraesthesia but no increase in serious cardiovascular events in the trials reviewed. The authors concluded that triptans are effective for acute migraine in adults and should be offered early in the attack. Source: Synthetic Headache Evidence Review. Location: Canada.
